## Session Overview

**Topic:** AI-powered web app for MBA research methodology assistance - a "research companion" that guides non-researchers through the entire academic research journey.

**Goals:**
- Make academic research accessible for professionals without research background
- Single-place platform covering all research stages (problématique to defense)
- AI agents assisting at each step: document discovery, literature review, methodology selection, analysis
- Keep it simple and guided, not overwhelming

**User Context:** Software engineer building for MBA students, new to research methodology domain

**Target User:** Industry professionals doing an MBA while working full-time. Not academics. Not tech people specifically. Real people with 2 days/week max, who want a good grade without becoming methodology experts.

## Organized Themes

### Theme 1: Core User Problems
Users need hand-holding, not a blank canvas. They want outcomes (good grade, good paper) without becoming methodology experts.

### Theme 2: Document Discovery & Literature Review
**The killer feature.** AI finds papers, scores relevance, highlights passages, enables quick review, drafts literature review. Transform 40 hours into 2 hours.

### Theme 3: Methodology Assistant
Method recommendation, interview guide builder, target profiles, outreach templates, scheduling tracker. Don't make users learn methodology—just tell them what to do.

### Theme 4: Data Analysis
Auto-transcription, theme detection, quote extraction, coding assistance. Raw interviews → organized insights automatically.

### Theme 5: Paper Assembly & Writing
Progress dashboard, section status, version history, consistency checking, export. 12 months of fragments → one coherent paper.

### Theme 6: UX Patterns
Drag/drop, slash commands, visual timeline, daily focus, completion dopamine, universal search, status indicators, progress bars, split view, chapter jumps.

### Theme 7: Differentiation
Context persistence, MBA-specific, guided workflow, progress visibility, real citations, all-in-one, proactive guidance.
